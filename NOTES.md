- Hidden dialer codes for 15 phone makers and every standard Android code, plus 26 mobile networks in the UK, US, Ireland, Germany, France, Spain and Italy
- Every code checked against your phone, rated Verified, Reported or Legacy, and rated for risk; risky codes hidden until Advanced mode
- 26 hardware tests with Run all and a Phone Health Report PDF, and device info with about 120 details
- 90 guides for Android 10 to 17 with your brand's steps, and 70 hidden settings shortcuts
- Debloat lists for nine brands, 55 ADB commands with undo, and optional Power mode with Shizuku
- 245 countries with dialling codes, emergency numbers and a dial-abroad helper
- Favourites, notes, collections, encrypted backup, a home screen widget, a Quick Settings tile and app shortcuts
- No adverts, no account and no internet access

Needs Android 10 or later. Download `CodePro.apk` below and open it to install.
