Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026. This policy covers the CodePro app for Android (package uk.jj.codepro) and this website. CodePro is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom, who can be reached by opening an issue on the project's GitHub page.

The short version

  • CodePro does not hold Android's internet permission. It cannot go online, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
  • Your favourites, notes, marks, collections and test results stay on your phone and are left out of cloud backups.
  • There are no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no analytics.

What stays on your phone

CodePro keeps your favourites, notes, worked or didn't-work marks, collections, recently opened codes, test results and settings in the app's private storage. Android's cloud backup and device transfer are switched off for CodePro. Settings, Erase everything removes all of it in one go.

Reports and backups

The Phone Health Report, the device report and backups are made only when you ask, and saved or shared where you choose. Backups are encrypted on the phone with your password (AES-256-GCM, with a key made from your password by PBKDF2 with 310,000 rounds). Without the password nobody can open them, including the developer.

Links to this website

Settings, About has Check for a newer version, Website and More apps. Each opens this site in your browser; CodePro itself never goes online.

Keeping and deleting your data

Use Settings, Erase everything to remove all of CodePro's records. Uninstalling CodePro deletes everything it holds.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request, correct or erase on a server. Everything is already in your hands.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no scripts beyond the app list. GitHub may keep standard web server logs, such as IP addresses, for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.
